## Painted-kawaii 3D models (2026-10-22)
All models in `assets/art/export/models/` (ship, crew, props, rocks, wreck, whale, siren, atoll, harbour,
fish) are original, built by script in Blender (`tools/blender/*.py`, sources in `assets/art/source/blender/`).
Colours and the painted look come from the game's own shaders (`assets/shaders/toon*.gdshader`, `sea.gdshader`).
The siren's song is original, generated by `tools/make_siren_song.py`.
